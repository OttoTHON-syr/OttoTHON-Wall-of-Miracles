Photo focus tool

Photos are cropped to fit a frame. This tool lets you choose which part of the photo stays in view. Nothing is uploaded: the photo stays in your browser.

  1. Choose the photo (the same one you uploaded in the editor).
  2. Drag the box over the part that should show. Arrow keys nudge it too.
  3. Copy the two numbers into Photo focus on that person or event in the editor.
